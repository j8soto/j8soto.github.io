CASE 01 · HUMAN-CENTERED DESIGN

HTE Project Design

VIEW CASE STUDY ↗

Designing a more engaging science-learning experience for fourth-grade students through stakeholder research, ideation, prototyping, and in-person testing.

User ResearchInterviewsPersonasTestingPrototyping

THE RESEARCH QUESTION

How might we make learning about energy more engaging for fourth graders?

The project started with the people—not the interface. The team worked with a teacher and student users, combined primary and secondary research, then translated what we learned into a hands-on learning experience.

The strongest solution wasn't more information. It was a better way for students to participate in the learning.

CASE 02 · PRODUCT SYSTEM

Trinity

VIEW CASE STUDY ↗

An AI-powered quality management system built to connect people, work, documents, inspections, equipment, and evidence in one operational experience.

Product DesignSystemsWorkflowAIAutomation

THE SYSTEM PROBLEM

Quality information is only useful when the right person can find the right evidence at the right moment.

I translated complex operational requirements into a connected product model instead of treating every record as a separate screen.
