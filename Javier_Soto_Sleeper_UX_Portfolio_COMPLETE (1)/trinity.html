CASE STUDY 02 · PRODUCT SYSTEM · AI

Making a complex quality system feel simple.

Trinity is an AI-powered quality management system concept designed to centralize operational workflows, documentation, scheduling, reporting, and evidence for complex work environments.

ROLEProduct / UX Designer
FOCUSSystems · Workflow · Information Architecture
PLATFORMDesktop + mobile-first
TOOLSFigma · AI · Prototyping
TRINITY · FLEET / OPERATIONAL DASHBOARD
01 / THE PROBLEM

Too much context lived in too many places.

Complex quality operations create a chain of people, work items, documents, inspections, equipment, approvals, and evidence. When those relationships are fragmented, users spend time searching for context instead of acting on it.

The product challenge was not simply to make individual screens cleaner. It was to create a system where each piece of information had a clear relationship to the work around it.

02 / INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Design the relationships first.

The product model connects the operational hierarchy: Company → Availability → Work Item → Work Specification → TIP → Inspection / Callout → OQE. This creates a traceable path from the larger job context down to the evidence produced by an individual action.

REAL TRINITY DESIGN · FLEET VIEW
The goal was progressive context: show users what matters now, then make the underlying evidence easy to reach.
03 / INTERFACE DESIGN

Surface the signal. Hide the noise.

The dashboard prioritizes active work, open TIPs, pending OQEs, AI validation, and fleet-level progress. Users can scan status first, then drill into the records behind it.

TRINITY · DARK MODE DASHBOARD
04 / WORKFLOW THINKING

One action should carry its context forward.

Rather than making users reconstruct a work history manually, Trinity's screens are designed around the relationships between records. A user can move from a ship or availability into the work, the TIP, the inspection, and the resulting OQE.

05 / AI + AUTOMATION

Automation should remove work—not remove control.

Trinity explores AI-driven automation for document processing, information retrieval, workflow generation, compliance checks, and repetitive administrative work. The UX challenge is to make automation understandable, reviewable, and connected to the underlying evidence.

Automate the repetitive

Reduce manual data entry, document handling, and repetitive workflow steps.

Keep the human in the loop

Make generated or validated information visible so users can review and act with confidence.

06 / WHAT I LEARNED

Complexity doesn't have to show up in the interface.

Trinity pushed me to think beyond individual screens and toward systems, relationships, and progressive disclosure. The best experience is not the one that exposes every capability—it is the one that helps the user understand what matters now and gives them a clear path to everything else.

This project is also where my interests in UX, AI, workflow automation, and product thinking intersect most directly.
