Trinity: making complex QA operations easier to navigate.

Trinity is a mobile-first AI-powered quality management system designed to centralize fragmented operational workflows, documentation, scheduling, reporting, personnel, and equipment information.

Product DesignUX / UIWorkflow DesignAIPrototyping
The product challenge: turn complex operational work into workflows that feel understandable to people with very different levels of technical experience.
01 / Problem

Fragmented work creates friction.

QA operations involve schedules, documentation, inspections, personnel qualifications, equipment, compliance, and reporting. Trinity was designed to bring those workflows into one connected product experience.

Design goal

Use familiar interaction patterns and clear information hierarchy so users can find what they need quickly instead of navigating disconnected spreadsheets, documents, and manual processes.

02 / Product architecture

Design around the work, not the org chart.

Operations

Dashboard, Fleet, Scheduling, and project status give users a shared operational picture.

Quality

Audit, compliance, documentation, and inspection workflows keep critical information connected.

People

Welding, personnel qualifications, certifications, and availability make staffing decisions easier.

03 / Interface system

Simple enough for daily use. Powerful enough for complex work.

The interface uses a restrained visual hierarchy, modular dashboards, clear status information, and task-oriented navigation. The goal is not to hide complexity, but to reveal it progressively when users need it.

04 / AI + automation

Reduce the administrative load.

Trinity incorporates AI and automation concepts for document processing, information retrieval, workflow generation, compliance checks, and repetitive administrative tasks.

05 / Outcome

A connected operating layer.

The product direction centralizes operational information and creates a consistent way to move from task → evidence → status → action. It is intended to improve visibility while reducing fragmented manual work.
